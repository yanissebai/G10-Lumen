Integrated recommendation · Role 1

Germany market entry

A measured launch built to learn.

LUMEN must balance premium brand-building with financial discipline despite having no observed German sales history. The evidence supports a staged launch with explicit validation gates.

Recommended opening move

Test €2.19 per can, lead with Gym & Office and DTC Online, focus initial validation on high-intent urban wellness and fitness audiences, and add Retail/Grocery selectively after validating repeat purchase and contribution.

Why €2.19

It retains 51.7% stated acceptance while materially improving per-unit contribution versus €1.79. At €2.59, acceptance falls to 26.7%.

Why these channels

At €2.19, contribution is €1.16 in DTC Online and €1.13 in Gym & Office, versus €0.63 in Retail/Grocery.

What to validate

Measure CAC, repeat purchase, fulfilment-adjusted contribution, and payback by channel before committing to national scale.

What this gives up

The launch accepts less initial reach than a retail-first plan and less unit margin than €2.59 in exchange for a stronger acceptance–economics balance.

Observed facts and calculations are documented in the integration analysis and the Role 3 pricing analysis. Survey identity fields are not used or exposed.
