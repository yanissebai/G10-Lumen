Implications for the final recommendation

What the team should carry forward

01 · BASE CASE

Model Germany at €2.19

Use the tested price as the base assumption, then validate actual sell-through, repeat purchase, and the €0.62 implied unit cost.

02 · AUDIENCE

Lead with wellness and fitness

Those segments have higher observed price thresholds. Broad student reach is a conscious non-objective at launch.

03 · CHANNELS

Keep channel choice separate

Pricing supports €2.19 across all three tests, but channel selection still needs reach, CAC, repeat behavior, and operational evidence.
